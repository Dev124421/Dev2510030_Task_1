Cyberpunk
2077

Cyberpunk 2077 is an action role-playing game played from a first-person perspective. You play a mercenary whose voice, face, hairstyle, body type, modifications, background and clothing are customisable.

PC / PS5 / XBOX SERIES X|S / NINTENDO SWITCH 2 / MAC

Regions

Watson

A dense, multicultural district featuring immigrant neighbourhoods, tight alleys and industrial zones.

Westbrook

A wealthy, high-end entertainment and residential district.

City Center

The gleaming corporate heart and financial centre of Night City.

Heywood

A large residential and multi-ethnic district controlled heavily by the Valentinos gang.

Santo Domingo

An old industrial and manufacturing district filled with power plants and factories.

Pacifica

An abandoned, lawless combat zone largely controlled by the Voodoo Boys gang.

Dogtown (Phantom Liberty)

A walled-off, ruined sub-district of Pacifica run by Kurt Hansen's Barghest militia.

The Badlands

The vast, arid desert and wasteland surrounding Night City, populated by Nomads.


Characters

V

The player character, a mercenary whose voice, face, hairstyle, body type, modifications, background and clothing are customisable.

Johnny Silverhand

A rockstar and anti-corporate terrorist who died in 2023 and was resurrected as a digital ghost in V's head. He is portrayed by Keanu Reeves.

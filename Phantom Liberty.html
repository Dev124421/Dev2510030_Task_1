Spy-Thriller Expansion

Phantom
Liberty

Phantom Liberty is a spy-thriller expansion for Cyberpunk 2077 set in Dogtown, a walled-off district of Night City. V is drawn into a conspiracy to rescue the president of the New United States of America, where every ally has an agenda.

PC / PS5 / XBOX SERIES X|S

Story

Space Force One Down

The presidential shuttle is shot down over Dogtown, and V is pulled into a high-stakes rescue mission.

A Failing Body

With the clock ticking, V must decide whom to trust and what price to pay for a chance at survival.

Choices Matter

A branching spy-thriller story where decisions change who makes it out of Dogtown alive.

Requires Base Game

Phantom Liberty is an expansion and requires Cyberpunk 2077 to play.


Dogtown

The District

A walled-off, ruined sub-district of Pacifica, once an unfinished entertainment complex.

Barghest

A militia led by Kurt Hansen that runs Dogtown by force.

Lawless Streets

Cramped alleys, abandoned stadium grounds and black markets with their own rules.

New Gear

New weapons, vehicles and cyberware to find across the district.


Characters

Solomon Reed

A former NUSA intelligence agent with a long history in Night City's shadows. He is portrayed by Idris Elba.

Songbird

A netrunner and covert operative who contacts V through a hidden channel. She is portrayed by Elodie Yung.

Rosalind Myers

The president of the New United States of America, trapped in Dogtown. She is portrayed by Candis Cayne.

Kurt Hansen

The commander of Barghest and the self-appointed ruler of Dogtown. He is portrayed by Peter Weller.
